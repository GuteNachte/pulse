## Homelable

Pulse's network-topology module contains adapted topology-editor concepts and source derived from Homelable.

- Project: https://github.com/Pouzor/homelable
- Version: `v3.1.1`
- Source commit: `d9f3b4c18ab8adb1be742abd45806e6bb302dbce`
- Adapted scope: side handles, waypoint edge behavior, canvas history, layout serialization, and their focused tests
- Excluded scope: Homelable backend, scanners, inventory, monitoring, import integrations, floor plans, and themes
